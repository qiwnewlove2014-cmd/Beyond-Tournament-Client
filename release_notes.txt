• Interactive Objects & Audio
- **Playable Piano:** Added a fully interactive, network-synced Piano! Players can now perform live music in real-time. We've designed a highly responsive custom keyboard layout for smooth playing across multiple octaves.
- **Dynamic Piano Acoustics:** The piano features a brand-new dynamic acoustic system. When you play or stand close to the piano, the sound envelopes you in a rich, wide stereo experience. As you walk away, the sound naturally and seamlessly transitions into a realistic 3D directional audio source, providing an incredibly immersive and natural listening experience for everyone around.
