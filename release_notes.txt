[v1.6.7] Classic Puppet Zombie & Builder Surface Expansion — 2026-08-09

• Zombie Mode & Enemies
- **Classic Puppet Zombie Miniboss:** Classic Puppet Zombies now silently infiltrate standard Zombie Mode waves from round 7 onward, replacing two or three ordinary zombies without increasing the total wave size or announcing their arrival.
- **Distinct Miniboss Audio and Movement:** Classic Zombies use their own ambient, attack, damage, and death sounds, move with normal surface-aware zombie footsteps, and bring stronger health and melee damage without magical boss shields or spell abilities.
- **Controlled Staff Testing:** Authorized staff can use `/spawnclassiczombie` to test up to three active Classic Zombies during a Zombie Mode match.

• Map Builder & World Audio
- **Separate Floor and Wall Tools:** The Builder now presents `Platform (Floor)` and `Wall` as separate top-level choices, keeping floor materials focused and making the expanded wall library easier to reach beside the other map-editing tools.
- **Expanded Surface Audio Library:** Added 17 floor materials with dedicated footsteps and 48 additional wall sounds, including bare ground, broken glass, caves, ceramic, clay, cloth, debris, decks, deep sand, deep snow, hardwood, mud, bridges, metal structures, foliage, containers, and many more environmental surfaces.
- **Consistent Editing Workflow:** Newly added floor and wall materials are available during placement and existing-element editing while older Builder menu requests remain compatible.
