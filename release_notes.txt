• UX, Menus & Input Safety
- **Menu Input Isolation & Voice Chat Safety:** Restored clean letter-search navigation across all overlay and weapon selection menus. Pressing navigation hotkeys while inside menus now functions strictly as menu navigation without accidentally activating Push-to-Talk voice channels.
- **Redundant Arcade Cabinet Exit Cleaned:** Streamlined the Arcade Cabinet interface to feature a single, clean Close button without duplicate exit options.

• Shield System & Combat Failsafes
- **Flexible Curved Arc & Physical Width Barrier:** Upgraded the shield defense engine with dynamic 3-stage combat barrier physics. Shields now feature configurable physical barrier widths and curved defense arcs that adapt dynamically to player rotation, supporting up to 360-degree dome protection.
- **Downed State Shield Lock:** Prevented players who are dead or awaiting revive from raising or lowering defensive shields until they are successfully revived by a teammate.

• Zombie Game & Sound Rotation
- **Weighted Sound Theme Rotation:** Re-engineered round sound theme selection to automatically rotate among available sound themes every round, utilizing probabilistic weight balancing to prevent consecutive folder repetition and ensure equal distribution across extended matches.

• Weapon Management & Auto-Balancing
- **Melee Weapon Auto-Balancing:** Expanded administrative balance tools to support Melee weapons, allowing staff to configure custom recoil parameters and auto-equalize target Damage Per Second (DPS) across both firearms and melee weapons.

• Audio Engine & PA Speakers
- **Automatic PA Speaker Initialization:** Map Public Address (PA) speakers now initialize automatically upon joining any server or map without requiring manual map reloading.

• System & Core Engine
- **Client & Server Protocol Sync:** Synchronized network protocol and version markers across Client and Server to v1.5.4.
