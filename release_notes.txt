[v1.7.5] Megaphone & PA Low-Latency Audio — 2026-08-13
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Audio Engine & Voice Chat
- **PA Now Feels Like Normal Voice Chat:** The megaphone/PA speakers no longer force a fixed 120ms jitter buffer + 60ms pre-buffer (180ms floor). A new adaptive jitter buffer starts at 20ms and only grows when the network actually shows jitter, so music, guitar relays, speech, and band performances come through the PA almost immediately - measured ~40ms on a clean network vs the old 180ms (4.5x faster), with zero crackle.
- **Smarter Spike Handling:** The jitter estimate uses a fast-attack peak-hold (WebRTC-style), so a stray delayed packet briefly widens the buffer and then decays back down - fewer dropouts on jittery networks than a fixed 20ms buffer, while still staying far below the old fixed latency.
- **One Rule for Everything:** Voice, music bot MP3, and live instrument relays all flow through the same adaptive receive path, so speech and songs benefit from the same low latency as instruments.
- **Real-Time Spatial Tracking:** The PA speaker volume/occlusion/cone targets are now recomputed continuously (10Hz) while the listener walks, instead of waiting for a camera-movement event, so the PA image follows you in real time without 'swaying on its own'. Occlusion now glides through a per-speaker EMA (fast attack / slow release) and the behind-speaker muffle ramps smoothly over a ~2-unit zone, so walking along walls or across a speaker's back no longer flips the filter instantly.
- **Long Sessions Stay Put:** Idle PA sources are kept for 30s (was 5s) and the acoustic delay baseline for 60s, so a quiet music passage or a long pause no longer tears down and rebuilds the speakers mid-song - the PA image no longer 'merges and splits' on its own.
- **Delay Tracks You Without Stuttering:** Propagation-delay resync now pads at most one silence frame per 20ms packet while walking (was: the whole difference at once - a 100-300ms hole), so the spatial stagger follows the listener smoothly instead of jumping.
- **Music + Talk No Longer Interleave:** The music bot, local mic, and instruments each get their OWN PA source set ('player:music' / 'player:mic' / 'player:guitar'). Simultaneous local streams (singing over the music, talking while playing guitar) now mix inside OpenAL instead of sharing one queue where 20ms slices interleaved - that shared queue used to receive 30ms of audio per 20ms, so the delay kept climbing while talking and both streams sounded stretched/squeezed with clicks. Each stream now keeps its own cadence: no delay buildup, no squeezing, zero added latency, and the music alone sounds exactly like before.
- **The PA Image No Longer Merges/Splits on Its Own:** The per-speaker propagation-delay baseline is now FROZEN at the stream's start position. Re-basing it on every walk step (and re-padding at the next recovery) made the inter-cabinet stagger flip between merged (same quantized 20ms frame) and separated (one frame apart) as the listener moved - standing between two loud cabinets, the image kept snapping between mono and stereo with no rhyme or reason. Movement now only affects the spatial GAINS (volume/occlusion refresh + per-frame LERP, still real-time); the delay stagger stays put, so the image is deterministic. Fresh streams and underrun recovery still rebuild the stagger from the frozen baseline, so it never jumps.
- **No More Latency Spikes When the Network Drops a Packet:** Voice, music bot, and megaphone audio is now sent as UNRELIABLE ENet packets on both client and server. Previously every audio packet was RELIABLE: a single lost frame stalled the whole stream behind an ENet retransmission (head-of-line blocking), turning one dropped packet into a 150-200ms latency spike for every subsequent frame - audible as periodic stutters mid-song on any lossy link. Now a lost frame simply drops (a single 20ms hole that the adaptive jitter buffer absorbs or the de-click padding hides), while the stream keeps flowing at the 40ms floor. Data packets (game logic) are untouched and stay reliable.
- **Live Cover Sync (Play Along with the Song, In Time):** When the song owner plays a track and a band member drums/piano/guitars along through the PA, the owner used to hear the cover ~100-300ms behind their own song - the performer can only hit after HEARING the song one network leg late, and their playing then travels a second leg back, while the owner's song itself plays through the zero-latency local monitor. The owner's local song monitor is now held back by 2RTT + 40ms (measured by an automatic 5s ping sampler, capped at 240ms) through a small FIFO in the shared local-feed path, so the song and the remote band line up at the owner's ears with ~0ms offset. The formula targets MIDI note-event instruments (piano/drums arrive via a jitter-free path = 2RTT+40ms); audio-stream instruments (guitar) stay within one 40ms jitter floor. The owner's OWN instruments stay instant, and future instruments opt in with one tag. Non-owner listeners keep the unavoidable 1-leg floor (the performer must hear the song before playing).
- **Normal Voice Chat Is Now As Fast As the PA:** The normal 3D voice channel padded EVERY cold-start burst with 5 x 20ms of silence (100ms) - so a guitarist's first strum after a pause, or any new sentence, landed a full 100ms late even on a clean LAN. The padding is now an adaptive per-channel jitter margin (same fast-attack / decay math the megaphone uses): a 20ms minimum that grows only while the network actually shows jitter and decays back on its own. A >180ms silence gap (a pause between strums, a breath) resets to the 20ms minimum instead of counting the silence as jitter. Clean-LAN voice/guitar drops from ~119ms to ~39ms (-80ms); under real jitter the margin still grows up to 120ms so crackle protection is unchanged.

[v1.7.4] Guitar & Music Bot PA Broadcasting Fix — 2026-08-13
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Multi-Performer Broadcasting (Band Mode)
- **Play as a Band, Not a Single Slot:** "Broadcast to Megaphone" is no longer one slot per person. Any number of staff can hold it at the same time, so a pianist, a drummer, and guitarists can all perform through the PA speakers together (duo / band jams). Every performer in the broadcast set gets PA routing for their piano, drums, and guitar - and the same rule applies to instruments added in the future.
- **Music Stays One Slot:** The MP3 stream keeps a single-owner slot, so two people's music never overlaps on the speakers. If you enable "Broadcast to Megaphone" while another performer already holds the music slot, the game announces it and your instruments still broadcast - your music stays private until the slot frees up, at which point it takes over automatically.
- **Talk Alongside the Band:** Performers broadcasting to the PA can still use the megaphone weapon to talk; the voice no longer gets blocked by another performer's broadcast lock (audio is equal-power mixed, nothing is hijacked).

• Music Bot & Megaphone Routing
- **Guitar Follows the Other Instruments Through the PA:** "Broadcast to Megaphone: ON" in the Music Bot menu is now an independent toggle for the guitar too - exactly like Piano and Drumset. Previously the guitar only mixed into the broadcast when the separate music broadcast (Alt+M) was also enabled, so a performer who turned on "Broadcast to Megaphone" alone heard piano/drums on the PA but never their guitar. The guitar now joins the PA/megaphone stream whenever the performer holds the Music Bot broadcast slot, with or without the music broadcast toggle.
- **No More Doubled Guitar Audio:** When the guitar is mixed into the Music Bot broadcast, it no longer also streams on the raw voice channel. Previously the same strum could be sent twice (voice channel + bot mix), doubling it for nearby players and through the PA.

• Bug Fixes & Stability
- **Live Guitar Relay Without a Track Playing:** The background live relay now keeps running when "Broadcast to Megaphone" is ON even when no music track is playing, so a strummed guitar is broadcast to the PA immediately instead of requiring the MP3 streamer to be active.
