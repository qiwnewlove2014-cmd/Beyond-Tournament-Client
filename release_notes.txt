[v1.5.9] Navigation Audio & Music Control Patch — 2026-08-02

• Navigation Audio & Input
- Optional Wall Proximity Tone provides a directional 3D cue for walls within five tiles.
- Tab direction check provides a spatial compass cue; turning cues and post-turn degree announcements are configurable and disabled by default.
- Turning sensitivity now has four persistent levels starting at the game's original turning rate and reaching Very Fast. Highlight it in Options and adjust directly with Left or Right; Enter stays in Options and Escape returns to gameplay.
- Direction check, spectator switching, and Pong spectator camera keys are configurable, including intentional overlapping bindings.

• Music Bot
- Personal Music Feed shuffles Favorites, supports previous/next controls, and can use normal Broadcast or authorized Megaphone routing.
- Fixed stale background music loads that could start after selecting another song or pressing Stop.
- Music Bot control, Music Bot volume, and Volume Mixer shortcuts can now be changed through `Configure key bindings`; existing defaults remain unchanged, modifier commands follow the selected Music Bot key, and the help menu announces the active bindings.

• Native Feedback
- Added an accessible Feedback and Bug Report center at the end of the authenticated Main Menu with Bug, Suggestion, and General categories.
- Feedback and replies are limited to 1,000 characters; new submissions have a 60-second per-account cooldown and replies have a 10-second cooldown, with independent Server validation and protected ownership checks.
- Moderator-level staff and above can review, reply to, and resolve Feedback inside the game; Builder-only accounts cannot access the review queue.
- Staff reply guidance is limited to two notices per Feedback, with online notifications limited to once per minute while every reply remains saved.
- Staff can resolve and archive answered Feedback without erasing player history, and review resolved items separately.
- Contributor/Admin-level staff can permanently remove resolved Feedback only after entering a reason and completing two confirmations; the Server requires an atomic audit record before deletion.
- Added a staff-only review guide covering respectful replies, privacy, fair treatment of criticism, escalation, and responsible permanent deletion.
- Removed the Discord integration so Feedback and staff notifications no longer depend on an external chat service.
- Fixed compiled Server packages missing required Pong JSON resources during startup.
- Fixed the staff `move` command clearing test items and gameplay state during same-map teleports; cross-map moves still reset mode-specific equipment.

• Staff AI
- Removed automated Mason AI broadcasts and the related broadcast toggle commands. Mason remains available for direct staff assistance.

🔒 Security & Client Diagnostics
- Caught client errors now create a verified diagnostic report with the error type and traceback, allowing staff to investigate real failures sooner.
- Normal exits and unexplained process closures no longer create staff warnings, preventing false security alerts during ordinary play or development testing.
