[v1.6.6] Persistent Builder Instruments & Piano Chorus — 2026-08-08

• Map Builder & Interactive Objects
- **Permanent Musical Instrument Placement:** Added a Musical Instruments submenu to the Map Builder. Builders can enter exact X, Y, and Z coordinates for a playable Piano and save it permanently into the map.
- **Multiple Piano Support:** Builder-placed pianos use unique map identities, allowing multiple playable pianos to coexist and participate in edit, copy, paste, delete, and hot-reload workflows.

• Interactive Objects & Audio
- **Synchronized Piano Chorus:** Press Tab while playing a piano to toggle a dimensional Chorus effect with a short wet fade. The performer hears it immediately and nearby listeners hear the synchronized effect in real time, including environmental reverb and PA/megaphone output.
