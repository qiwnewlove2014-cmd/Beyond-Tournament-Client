[v1.8.1] Core Audio Engine, Jam Latency Sync & Accessibility Enhancements — 2026-08-25
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Core Audio Engine & Spatial Acoustic Optimization
- **Dynamic Audio Hardware Watchdog & Safe Device Recovery:** Enhanced the real-time audio device monitoring system to gracefully handle external USB sound cards, Bluetooth headphones, and Windows audio endpoint transitions. If an active audio device is disconnected or suspended by power-saving states, the engine seamlessly recovers and routes playback to the system default audio device without interrupting the game loop or raising exception alerts.
- **High-Precision 3D Spatial Acoustic Raycasting:** Re-engineered 3D Euclidean distance calculations within the line-of-sight acoustic raycasting pipeline. This eliminates excessive iteration overhead in multi-story buildings, delivers noticeable client-side performance gains, and ensures pinpoint accuracy for environmental sound muffling and physical wall occlusion across all map elevations.
- **In-Game Soft Audio Recovery:** Added `Refresh game audio` to the in-game Options menu. It safely resumes the current room ambience and map music, re-evaluates nearby spatial sound sources, restores room Reverb sends, refreshes Music Bot and Megaphone output, requests an authoritative Jukebox resync, and restores Master gain without disconnecting, moving the song position, or rebuilding the OpenAL context. The action remains in Options, has a five-second cooldown, and directs players to the existing Restart Client fallback when the output device itself is unavailable. Both player guides include the complete accessible workflow and limitations.

• Music Jam Session & Latency Compensation
- **Server-Side Timestamp for Instrument Notes:** Piano, guitar, and drum note payloads now include a server_time timestamp (Date.now()). This allows clients to estimate clock offset and synchronize instrument playback across all listeners, which is essential for blind-accessible music jam sessions where visual cues cannot be used.
- **Client-Side Clock Synchronization:** Added exponential moving average (EMA) clock offset calculation that compares server_time with local time, using measured RTT to estimate one-way delay. The offset is smoothed with alpha=0.3 to filter network jitter and provide stable timing reference for all instrument notes.
- **Latency-Compensated Note Playback:** Piano, guitar, and drum notes are now delayed by the calculated clock offset plus jukebox jitter buffer (~200ms) before playback. This ensures that remote instrument notes arrive at listeners in sync with the jukebox audio, eliminating the ~160-180ms desynchronization that previously occurred between music and instruments.

• Packet Validation & Player Radar
- **Player Radar Fully Repaired (Y Key):** Pressing Y always answered "There is no one in a five tile radius of you" even with players standing right next to you. The packet schema validator registered player_radar with an empty field list, which silently stripped the radius value from every radar request before the handler ran — the radius then computed as NaN and no player could ever pass the distance check. The schema now declares the radius field (integer 1–32), which also blocks tampered clients from requesting map-wide player scans.
- **Nearby-Player Detection Made 100% Reliable:** The player radar and drop menu (Alt+Y) now locate nearby players by iterating the map's authoritative player set instead of the player quadtree. The quadtree accumulated stale and duplicate entries as players moved (remove/re-insert on every step), so proximity lookups intermittently missed players standing in plain sight. Alt+Y kept working only because it hard-codes its radius server-side, which masked the radar defect.
- **Wallbuy Weapon Slot Selection Restored:** Buying a weapon from a wallbuy ignored the weapon slot the player had selected. The interact schema did not declare the selected_slot field, so the validator stripped it from every interact packet and the server always fell back to the active weapon slot. The field is now validated (integer -1 to 3) and slot placement works again, including placing a new weapon into the empty fourth slot.
- **Band Music Timeline Sync Restored:** Remote listeners hearing piano, drum, and guitar performances lost the precise shared-timeline scheduling, subtly desynchronizing ensemble play. The instrument schemas declared the music sync field under the wrong name (music_sync_ts, a number) while clients actually send music_sync (a timeline object), so the validator stripped the marker from every note. The schema now passes the object through, and the server's strict validatedMusicSync checks (version, epoch, frame sequence, bounded timing window) remain fully enforced.

• Pong Minigame & Acoustic Spatial Enhancements
- **Sweet Spot Center Drive & Slicing Mechanics:** Added dynamic hit mechanics to the paddle system. Hitting the ball with the center of the paddle unleashes a high-velocity Sweet Spot Drive (+25% forward speed) with laser-straight trajectory, while hitting with the outer edges or slicing into the ball angles sharp cross-court cuts across the table.
- **Full-Table Acoustic Coverage & Reverb Attenuation:** Re-engineered spatial audio and distance attenuation curves for the ball rolling loop. The sound is now smoothly audible across the entire 22-block table diagonal without abrupt drop-offs, while dynamically suppressing auxiliary room reverb and direct sound outside the arena to prevent ambient acoustic leakage into adjacent rooms.

• Combat & Interaction
- **Grenades Can No Longer Be "Thrown" After Exploding:** A grenade that exploded in the player's hand (pin pulled but not thrown in time) left its entity lingering on the map for several seconds. Pressing the interact key during that window replayed the throw sound and looping fuse hiss at the blast spot as if the grenade were thrown again, and the dead entity swallowed interactions meant for doors and wallbuys nearby. An exploded grenade now ignores interaction entirely and no longer blocks other objects from being used.

• Match Announcements
- **Death Announcements Restricted To Matches:** When a player died outside a match (in the world map or lobby), the "X just died!" announcement was broadcast to every player on the entire map — including people not in any match — and queued silently into their match buffer, surfacing minutes later as stale lines like "icesound just died!, 1 minute ago". Death announcements are now sent only to players inside the same match as the victim; deaths outside matches are no longer announced.

• New Drum Kit

Includes two snare pads (Snare 1 + Snare 2), a dedicated Rim pad, and enhanced volume scaling across all pads for a louder, punchier drumming experience. Available via Builder > Place Instrument > Drumset - Diw Kit.

• Bug Fixes
- **Fixed Alt+F4 Force Quit Crash:** Players pressing Alt+F4 would crash with an unhandled exception. Fixed the exit callback routing in force-quit handlers to safely clean up the application loop. Only affects players who press Alt+F4 (not F4 alone).

• Safe Map Archive and Recovery
- **Recoverable Map Removal:** Builder rank and higher may submit a Safe Archive request from the Builder Menu after hiding a non-core map from Matchmaking. The workflow records a reason and requires an exact map-name plus one-time-code confirmation; no permanent-delete button is exposed.
- **Independent Developer Review:** A different Developer account must approve the request. The Server repeats every role and selection check, rejects self-approval, and blocks protected core maps, visible maps, occupied maps, active/waiting match references, missing files, and unsafe names even if a Client packet is modified.
- **Checksum-Verified Restore:** Approved maps are atomically moved out of the live `maps` folder into runtime Archive storage with SHA-256 metadata and a 30-day retention marker. Developer Restore validates the checksum and XML, refuses name conflicts, and reloads the map into the live Server registry without requiring a full-data rollback.
- **Auditing and Backup Coverage:** Requests, approvals, rejections, Archive completion, and Restore completion are recorded in the Archive audit trail and Server system log. Full Server backups now include Safe Archive storage.
- **Builder Visibility Permission Preserved:** `Toggle Matchmaking Visibility` remains available to Builder rank and higher exactly as before and is kept separate from Archive approval or Restore authority.
- **Controlled Sole Developer Override:** When the registered Developer list contains exactly one account, that Developer may complete self-review only through two different one-time confirmation codes and a mandatory 30-second delay. The Server rechecks the registered list and every normal map safeguard at the final stage and marks the Archive metadata and audit trail explicitly; another Developer merely being offline never enables the override.
- **Strict Staff Map-Removal Policy:** The common section of `/staffpolicy` now includes a detailed Map Archive and Removal Policy for every staff role, covering authority, mandatory verification, prohibited manual deletion or audit tampering, Sole Developer rules, retention, recovery, incident response, escalation, and consequences for deliberate bypass.
- **Exact Confirmation Guide:** The shared policy, `/builderhelp`, and `/devhelp` now show accessible examples for Request, normal Developer approval, both Sole Developer stages, and Restore. The guide explains underscores, capitalization, spacing, one-time expiry, and how to restart safely after a mismatch without ever reusing an old code.

• Multi-Game Arcade Cabinet
- **Different Games Can Run Together:** A Multi-Game Arcade Cabinet may now host one session of each game type at the same time. For example, Pong and Blackjack can run from the same cabinet without blocking one another.
- **Duplicate Sessions Remain Protected:** The Server still prevents a second session of the same game type from starting at the same cabinet, while waiting multiplayer sessions remain joinable.
- **Cabinet-Specific Blackjack Routing:** Blackjack create and join actions now retain the authoritative cabinet identity, so players are not sent to an open table at another cabinet and packet-provided coordinates cannot override the map position.
- **Reliable Session Cleanup:** Blackjack no longer registers the same game twice and now removes its Server game entry when the arcade session ends, preventing stale rooms from blocking later play.
- **Return Beside the Cabinet:** Destroying or leaving an arcade match now returns each character to their own nearest safe pre-game position beside the cabinet instead of falling back to the map's main spawn point.
- **Future Game Support:** Cabinet occupancy uses an explicit game-type identity shared by Pong and Blackjack. Future arcade games can join the same isolation model without adding another cabinet-wide lock.
- **Automatic Accessible Zone Names:** Placing a Multi-Game, Pong, or Blackjack table through Builder now creates a matching named Zone automatically, consistent with Wallbuys and Musical Instruments. Deleting the table also removes its associated Zone.

World/all inventory reset, raw map replacement, leaderboard reset, restore, and account reset retain explicit confirmation steps appropriate to their impact.
- **Server-Validated Weapon Events:** Every weapon editor, balancing, and creation event now repeats its role check on the Server and validates weapon names, editable fields, numeric limits, creation stages, and output filenames. Injected menu packets cannot select unknown files, traverse paths, or create weapons outside the approved lowercase naming format.
- **Trusted Technician Access Repaired:** Temporary Technician permission now opens the same approved audio, PA, instrument, and megaphone maintenance paths as permanent Technician membership; the description no longer incorrectly promises Builder map tools.

• Client Restart & Complete Audio Recovery
- **Restart Client Button:** Added an accessible Restart Client option immediately before Exit on the main menu. After confirmation, the Client fades all audio, marks the shutdown as expected, hands off the single-instance lock, closes the old process, and waits for it to terminate before the replacement initializes Pygame or OpenAL. This provides a complete reset for stuck audio sources, buffers, devices, network state, MIDI, and background workers without generating a false unclean-exit crash report. If the replacement cannot launch, the current Client restores its crash-session marker and safely returns to the main menu.
