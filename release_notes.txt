[v1.6.8] Playable Drumset & Extensible MIDI Instruments — 2026-08-10

• Interactive Objects & Live Music
- **Playable 17-Piece Drumset:** Added a complete keyboard-playable drum kit with kick, snare, rim, three hi-hat articulations, four toms, crashes, china, splash, ride, ride bell, and cowbell. The accessible control layout works consistently with normalized Thai and English keyboard input.
- **Custom Drum Key Bindings:** Added a dedicated accessible settings menu where every drum sound can have a primary and optional alternate computer key, enabling techniques such as double kick and alternating snare strokes. Spoken conflict warnings, safe exit-key protection, per-pad alternate clearing, and one-step default restoration are included, while MIDI mappings remain independent and unchanged.
- **Reliable In-Game Drum Key Menu Navigation:** Fixed Escape navigation while configuring drum keys from the in-game Options menu. Canceling a key capture and backing out now returns one menu level at a time without reopening, swapping, or stacking stale configuration screens.
- **Responsive Percussion Performance:** Drum hits are predicted instantly for the performer, support controlled one-shot polyphony for natural rolls and cymbal tails, and use realistic closed/foot hi-hat choking without cutting unrelated sounds.
- **Spatial Drum Acoustics:** Nearby listeners hear balanced dual-source 3D drums with environmental reverb, wall occlusion, distance tuning, and optional Server-authorized PA/megaphone routing.
- **Safe Multiplayer Sessions:** The Server validates drum sessions, pad identifiers, instrument proximity, and a bounded performance rate before broadcasting authoritative drum positions to other listeners.
- **Persistent Drumset Placement:** Builders can place permanent Drumsets from the existing Musical Instruments menu with accessible coordinate suggestions, hot reload support, and unique identities for multiple kits on the same map.
- **Temporary Drumset Testing:** Moderators and authorized Builders can spawn a temporary Drumset nearby for live testing without saving it to the map.
- **Velocity-Sensitive MIDI Drums:** MIDI keyboards and electronic drum kits are detected automatically while playing, with General MIDI percussion support plus a compact C4-E5 layout covering all 17 pads.
- **Extensible MIDI Instrument Support:** MIDI devices now use one safe shared input hub with instrument profiles, reliable hot-plug cleanup, and protected switching between Piano, Drumset, and future playable instruments.
- **Selectable Drum Sound Kits:** Drumsets can now use different sound kits. A second "Salamander" acoustic kit (CC-BY 3.0, by Alexander Holm) is available alongside the default kit. Builders pick the kit when placing a Drumset from the Musical Instruments menu, the chosen kit persists in the map, and each player hears the performer's real kit. Existing maps keep using the default kit automatically. Tom 3 and Tom 4 of the Salamander kit are intentionally silent because that kit only ships two toms.
