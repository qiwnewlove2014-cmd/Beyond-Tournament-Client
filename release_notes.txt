[v1.6.9] Drum Kit Selection & Natural Instrument Acoustics — 2026-08-10

• Interactive Objects & Live Music
- **Selectable Drum Sound Kits:** Drumsets can now use different sound kits. A second "Salamander" acoustic kit (CC-BY 3.0, by Alexander Holm) is available alongside the default kit. Builders pick the kit when placing a Drumset from the Musical Instruments menu, the chosen kit persists in the map, and each player hears the performer's real kit. Existing maps keep using the default kit automatically. Tom 3 and Tom 4 of the Salamander kit are intentionally silent because that kit only ships two toms.
- **Natural Instrument Distance Fade:** Playable instruments (Piano, Drumset) now fade out smoothly and naturally for nearby listeners as they walk away, reaching true silence at about 50 steps. Previously the sound stayed audible at a fixed low volume far away and then cut off abruptly at the edge.
