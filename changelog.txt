[v1.7.7] Natural World Map Animals & Spatial Audio Polish — 2026-08-14
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Audio Engine & Voice Chat
- **Natural Distance Fade for Entity Sounds:** Footsteps and vocal calls from horses, boars, zombies, and other players now fade out smoothly with distance and reach true silence at about 40 steps, instead of staying faintly audible from far away and then cutting off abruptly.
- **Horse Hoofbeat Range:** Horse walk/run hoof sounds are now only heard within 40 steps, so a horse moving inside a distant stable no longer echoes across the whole map.

• Monster & Boss Entities
- **Calmer Animal Vocalizations:** World Map animals now space out their cries randomly between 30 seconds and 1 minute instead of calling every few seconds, and each animal rolls its own staggered first call so a herd never cries at the same time.
- **Animals Stay Inside Pens:** World Map animals can no longer slip through fence corners or leap over pen walls while wandering or fleeing, so horses and boars stay inside their stables.
- **Natural Animal Behaviour:** World Map animals walk, run, rest, and graze at their own randomized pace, stay close to where they were released, and sprint away when spooked.

• Staff & Builder Tools
- **Round Up Animals (/penall):** New staff command rounds up every horse and boar on the World Map into its Animal Pen zone(s). Aliases include /roundup, /herd, /corral, and /catchall; available to Builder staff and above.
- **Invisible Animal Pen Zones:** Builders can place an invisible "Animal Pen" zone from the Builder menu — animals standing inside survive server restarts and monster-clearing commands.
- **World Map Spawn Rules:** Only horses can be spawned on World Maps by non-Developers; all other spawn commands (bosses, boar, piano, drumset, motorcycle) require Developer there, while matches keep the normal permissions.
- **Flexible Instrument Placement:** Pianos and drum sets now place within 2 steps of the builder and never overlap each other.

[v1.7.5] Megaphone & PA Low-Latency Audio — 2026-08-13
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Audio Engine & Voice Chat
- **PA Now Feels Like Normal Voice Chat:** The megaphone/PA speakers no longer force a fixed 120ms jitter buffer + 60ms pre-buffer (180ms floor). A new adaptive jitter buffer starts at 20ms and only grows when the network actually shows jitter, so music, guitar relays, speech, and band performances come through the PA almost immediately - measured ~40ms on a clean network vs the old 180ms (4.5x faster), with zero crackle.
- **Smarter Spike Handling:** The jitter estimate uses a fast-attack peak-hold (WebRTC-style), so a stray delayed packet briefly widens the buffer and then decays back down - fewer dropouts on jittery networks than a fixed 20ms buffer, while still staying far below the old fixed latency.
- **One Rule for Everything:** Voice, music bot MP3, and live instrument relays all flow through the same adaptive receive path, so speech and songs benefit from the same low latency as instruments.
- **Real-Time Spatial Tracking:** The PA speaker volume/occlusion/cone targets are now recomputed continuously (10Hz) while the listener walks, instead of waiting for a camera-movement event, so the PA image follows you in real time without 'swaying on its own'. Occlusion now glides through a per-speaker EMA (fast attack / slow release) and the behind-speaker muffle ramps smoothly over a ~2-unit zone, so walking along walls or across a speaker's back no longer flips the filter instantly.
- **Long Sessions Stay Put:** Idle PA sources are kept for 30s (was 5s) and the acoustic delay baseline for 60s, so a quiet music passage or a long pause no longer tears down and rebuilds the speakers mid-song - the PA image no longer 'merges and splits' on its own.
- **Delay Tracks You Without Stuttering:** Propagation-delay resync now pads at most one silence frame per 20ms packet while walking (was: the whole difference at once - a 100-300ms hole), so the spatial stagger follows the listener smoothly instead of jumping.
- **Music + Talk No Longer Interleave:** The music bot, local mic, and instruments each get their OWN PA source set ('player:music' / 'player:mic' / 'player:guitar'). Simultaneous local streams (singing over the music, talking while playing guitar) now mix inside OpenAL instead of sharing one queue where 20ms slices interleaved - that shared queue used to receive 30ms of audio per 20ms, so the delay kept climbing while talking and both streams sounded stretched/squeezed with clicks. Each stream now keeps its own cadence: no delay buildup, no squeezing, zero added latency, and the music alone sounds exactly like before.
- **The PA Image No Longer Merges/Splits on Its Own:** The per-speaker propagation-delay baseline is now FROZEN at the stream's start position. Re-basing it on every walk step (and re-padding at the next recovery) made the inter-cabinet stagger flip between merged (same quantized 20ms frame) and separated (one frame apart) as the listener moved - standing between two loud cabinets, the image kept snapping between mono and stereo with no rhyme or reason. Movement now only affects the spatial GAINS (volume/occlusion refresh + per-frame LERP, still real-time); the delay stagger stays put, so the image is deterministic. Fresh streams and underrun recovery still rebuild the stagger from the frozen baseline, so it never jumps.
- **No More Latency Spikes When the Network Drops a Packet:** Voice, music bot, and megaphone audio is now sent as UNRELIABLE ENet packets on both client and server. Previously every audio packet was RELIABLE: a single lost frame stalled the whole stream behind an ENet retransmission (head-of-line blocking), turning one dropped packet into a 150-200ms latency spike for every subsequent frame - audible as periodic stutters mid-song on any lossy link. Now a lost frame simply drops (a single 20ms hole that the adaptive jitter buffer absorbs or the de-click padding hides), while the stream keeps flowing at the 40ms floor. Data packets (game logic) are untouched and stay reliable.
- **Live Cover Sync (Play Along with the Song, In Time):** When the song owner plays a track and a band member drums/piano/guitars along through the PA, the owner used to hear the cover ~100-300ms behind their own song - the performer can only hit after HEARING the song one network leg late, and their playing then travels a second leg back, while the owner's song itself plays through the zero-latency local monitor. The owner's local song monitor is now held back by 2RTT + 40ms (measured by an automatic 5s ping sampler, capped at 240ms) through a small FIFO in the shared local-feed path, so the song and the remote band line up at the owner's ears with ~0ms offset. The formula targets MIDI note-event instruments (piano/drums arrive via a jitter-free path = 2RTT+40ms); audio-stream instruments (guitar) stay within one 40ms jitter floor. The owner's OWN instruments stay instant, and future instruments opt in with one tag. Non-owner listeners keep the unavoidable 1-leg floor (the performer must hear the song before playing).
- **Normal Voice Chat Is Now As Fast As the PA:** The normal 3D voice channel padded EVERY cold-start burst with 5 x 20ms of silence (100ms) - so a guitarist's first strum after a pause, or any new sentence, landed a full 100ms late even on a clean LAN. The padding is now an adaptive per-channel jitter margin (same fast-attack / decay math the megaphone uses): a 20ms minimum that grows only while the network actually shows jitter and decays back on its own. A >180ms silence gap (a pause between strums, a breath) resets to the 20ms minimum instead of counting the silence as jitter. Clean-LAN voice/guitar drops from ~119ms to ~39ms (-80ms); under real jitter the margin still grows up to 120ms so crackle protection is unchanged.

[v1.7.4] Guitar & Music Bot PA Broadcasting Fix — 2026-08-13
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Multi-Performer Broadcasting (Band Mode)
- **Play as a Band, Not a Single Slot:** "Broadcast to Megaphone" is no longer one slot per person. Any number of staff can hold it at the same time, so a pianist, a drummer, and guitarists can all perform through the PA speakers together (duo / band jams). Every performer in the broadcast set gets PA routing for their piano, drums, and guitar - and the same rule applies to instruments added in the future.
- **Music Stays One Slot:** The MP3 stream keeps a single-owner slot, so two people's music never overlaps on the speakers. If you enable "Broadcast to Megaphone" while another performer already holds the music slot, the game announces it and your instruments still broadcast - your music stays private until the slot frees up, at which point it takes over automatically.
- **Talk Alongside the Band:** Performers broadcasting to the PA can still use the megaphone weapon to talk; the voice no longer gets blocked by another performer's broadcast lock (audio is equal-power mixed, nothing is hijacked).

• Music Bot & Megaphone Routing
- **Guitar Follows the Other Instruments Through the PA:** "Broadcast to Megaphone: ON" in the Music Bot menu is now an independent toggle for the guitar too - exactly like Piano and Drumset. Previously the guitar only mixed into the broadcast when the separate music broadcast (Alt+M) was also enabled, so a performer who turned on "Broadcast to Megaphone" alone heard piano/drums on the PA but never their guitar. The guitar now joins the PA/megaphone stream whenever the performer holds the Music Bot broadcast slot, with or without the music broadcast toggle.
- **No More Doubled Guitar Audio:** When the guitar is mixed into the Music Bot broadcast, it no longer also streams on the raw voice channel. Previously the same strum could be sent twice (voice channel + bot mix), doubling it for nearby players and through the PA.

• Bug Fixes & Stability
- **Live Guitar Relay Without a Track Playing:** The background live relay now keeps running when "Broadcast to Megaphone" is ON even when no music track is playing, so a strummed guitar is broadcast to the PA immediately instead of requiring the MP3 streamer to be active.

[v1.7.2] Voice Chat, Megaphone Routing & Hotfixes — 2026-08-12
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Voice Chat & Megaphone Routing
- **Zero-Latency Megaphone Feedback:** Speaking into the PA/Megaphone system now routes your voice locally with absolute zero latency. You no longer have to wait for the network jitter buffer to hear your own voice, ensuring it instantly matches the responsiveness of playable instruments.
- **Instrument Megaphone Broadcasting:** External instrument input (Guitar/Bass line-in) now seamlessly routes through the Megaphone speakers at zero latency whenever you have the Megaphone active, making live stage performances and announcements much easier to manage.
- **Stereo Microphone Fallback:** The voice chat input device menu now fully supports Microphone Arrays and USB stereo microphones. Input devices that strictly require stereo capture will automatically fall back to stereo and be mixed down to mono in real time, preventing silent or failed microphone connections.

• Multi-Speaker Megaphone
- **Several People Can Talk at Once:** The megaphone is no longer limited to one speaker at a time. Multiple staff members, PA Test Mode users, and megaphone holders can broadcast through the PA simultaneously - the client sums every active speaker with equal-power mixing (1/√N), so talking together no longer clips or drops anyone's voice.
- **Voice Never Hijacks the PA:** The megaphone broadcast owner slot is reserved for the Music Bot only, so talking while someone is performing never yanks piano/drum PA routing away from them.
- **Guitar Notes Join the PA Jam Session:** Guitar/Bass notes now follow the same rule as Piano and Drumset - they route through the PA whenever the performer is actively using the megaphone (talked on it within the last 1.5s) or holds the Music Bot broadcast slot. Multiple guitarists can now perform through the PA at the same time instead of being limited to the single owner slot.

• Bug Fixes & Stability
- **Megaphone Voice Drop Fix:** Fixed a critical bug where the voice chat encoder would silently drop audio packets when speaking into the Megaphone, resulting in silent broadcasts.
- **No More Crackling PA Audio:** Restored the megaphone jitter buffer margins (60ms pre-buffer + 120ms margin) that had been cut down to 20ms, which made the PA audio break/crackle as soon as the network jitter exceeded 20ms.
- **Portable Announcements Restored:** Fixed an issue where the Music Bot's portable broadcast feature unintentionally muted microphones. Staff can once again make portable voice announcements anywhere on the map via the Music Bot menu.
- **Local PA Echo Fix:** Fixed a bug where performers playing the Piano or Drumset via the Megaphone could not hear their own instruments echoing out of the PA speakers around them. Local performers now hear exactly what the audience hears.
- **No More Doubled Music for the DJ:** The server no longer echoes a Music Bot broadcast back to its own performer, so the DJ no longer hears their music twice through comb filtering.
- **O Key No Longer Forces Megaphone Routing:** Pressing O (PA Test Mode) previously auto-routed a broadcasting Music Bot to the PA and acquired the megaphone lock, even when the performer never enabled "Broadcast to Megaphone: ON". The O key now controls PA Test Mode (voice) only - "Broadcast to Megaphone" is an independent toggle owned by the Music Bot menu and is never forced ON or OFF by the O key.

[v1.7.0] Client Stability, Key Bindings, Guitar Input & Instrument Fixes — 2026-08-11
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Stability & Crash Fixes
- **Fixed Map-Loading Crashes:** Resolved a frequent client crash (reported as unclean exits in `client_crashes`) that occurred when entering, changing, or building maps that contained Pianos and Drumsets. The root cause was remote piano note playback running on the network thread, where the audio (OpenAL) context is not current; under load this caused a native crash with no Python traceback.
- **Piano Audio Now Runs on the Main Thread:** All remote piano events (note play, note stop, soft pedal, pitch bend, chorus, and the piano note preloader) now queue to the main audio thread before touching audio, mirroring the safe pattern already used by the drum kit.
- **No More Instrument Memory Leaks Across Maps:** Switching maps now releases preloaded Piano and Drumset sample buffers. Previously, every map change kept the previous map's instrument buffers in memory, accumulating until the game crashed after long sessions.
- **Safer Audio Error Handling:** Hardened the shared spatial audio path with exception guards around OpenAL source, filter, and effect calls so that a transient audio fault is recovered instead of crashing the client.

• Keyboard Bindings & Settings
- **Enter Now Means Enter:** The interact key is now bound to the main Enter key by default, and every place that shows a key name (the key binding menu, the drum key menu, and travel point hints) speaks friendly names like "enter" instead of "return", so the key you hear always matches the key you press.
- **Main Enter vs Numpad Enter No Longer Swap:** Fixed a bug where a numpad Enter binding could be saved as the main Enter key, or fail to load at all; each Enter key now keeps its own binding through save and reload.
- **Clear Drum Keys:** The Configure drum keys menu now has a "Clear drum keys" option that wipes every custom drum binding at once so you can set them up fresh, separate from "Restore default drum keys" which brings back the factory layout.

• Live Music & Guitar Input
- **Second Audio Input for Instruments:** Added a "Select instrument input device" setting right beside the microphone input. The instrument line-in gets its own capture device and can run at the same time as the voice mic, so you can talk while you play.
- **Line-In Guitar & Bass (Press U):** Press U to toggle Guitar mode and plug your guitar or bass into the instrument input. The game pitch-detects what you play (low-note friendly for bass), converts it into notes, and plays them back at your position so you hear yourself instantly.
- **Guitar Notes Broadcast Like Piano:** Detected notes are sent to the server, which validates each note, clamps velocity to 1-127, rate-limits spam (30 notes/second), and broadcasts them to everyone on the map through the same note pipeline the piano and drums use.
- **Chord Strumming Heard Nearby:** Raw strum and chord audio (not just single notes) streams on the normal 3D voice channel, so players near the performer hear the full guitar sound with no broadcast toggle and no special permission.
- **Guitar Mixes Into the Music Bot Broadcast:** While the music bot broadcast is enabled, guitar audio is mixed into the broadcast stream (megaphone or the 3D music bot channel) so the whole area hears the performance.
- **USB Effects Pedal Support:** USB multi-effects pedals and amp modellers (Boss GT, Zoom G, Line 6 POD/Helix, Valeton, NUX, Mooer, Kemper, Axe-Fx, DigiTech, Vox, and more) are auto-detected when plugged in. Pedals that only expose stereo capture fall back to stereo input with automatic mono downmixing, and the instrument input menu lists likely guitar/pedal devices first, tagged "(guitar/pedal)".
