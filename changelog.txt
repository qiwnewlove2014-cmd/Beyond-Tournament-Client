BEYOND TOURNAMENT - CHANGELOG v1.5.5
================================================================================

• Jupiter Boss & Polymorphic Entity System
- **Jupiter Mini-Boss Infiltration:** Introduced Jupiter boss entities seamlessly into standard Zombie Game Mode rounds starting from Round 5+. Features 10,000 Base HP scaling, 3D arrival announcement roar, isolated spatial audio, and administrative spawn commands.
- **Polymorphic Audio Engine:** Expanded the entity sound engine to support custom entity sound folders dynamically, suppressing default surface tile footsteps for special boss entities while enabling 3D spatial charge windups and hit sound effects.

• Jupiter Boss AI & Tactical Maneuvers
- **Dynamic 25-Tactic Combat Matrix:** Upgraded Jupiter with an advanced AI tactical decision engine featuring 25 dynamic combat maneuvers. Includes S-curve sweeps, Z-zigzag rushes, spiral envelopes, shield-counter pivots, cover hugging, and 3-second Double Time Sprint bursts with distinct spatial footstep audio.
- **Proactive Melee & Ranged Evasion:** Jupiter now senses incoming melee weapons and firearms in real-time, executing smooth sidesteps and backsteps out of strike zones. During InstaKill mode, Jupiter gains heavy damage resistance (25% max HP cap per hit) and ultra-responsive evasion.
- **Mobile Ultimate Thunderstorm:** Transformed Jupiter's 4-strike thunderbolt ultimate into a mobile casting skill. Jupiter now continues running, sprinting, and chasing targets at full speed while raining down 360-degree lightning strikes.
- **Smooth Arc Trajectory & Audio Panning:** Enhanced AI movement physics with smooth angular LERP curve interpolation, eliminating sharp turn snaps and producing fluid 3D spatial audio panning.
- **Heavy Melee Stagger & Reactive Backstep:** Heavy melee weapon impacts now cause bosses to flinch with a 3D audio groan and sprint back 6–8 tiles, breaking heavy combos while dynamic evasion scales smoothly with weapon reach.
- **Dynamic Multi-Target Engagement:** Upgraded target selection to dynamically transfer aggro to active damage dealers in real-time, providing complete combat freedom during multi-player engagements.
- **Spawn Observation Stance & Sound Boost:** Bosses now observe players for 3 seconds upon arrival before initiating aggressive attacks, while footstep audio is boosted for clear 3D tactical tracking.
- **Dedicated Boss Special Rounds:** Introduced dedicated boss special rounds appearing via probabilistic evaluation (35% chance every 4+ rounds). Features custom announcement broadcasts, clean text formatting, 3D directional vector-boosted spatial arrival roars, and victory rewards (1,000 Bonus Points + Max Ammo + Double Points).
- **Tactical Stalking & Behavioral Variety:** Reduced close-range direct rush frequency, introduced tactical stalking pacing, feint-and-strike counter maneuvers, and a 2.8s–4.5s stance duration window to create organic mind games without constant chasing.
- **Enforced Spawn Stance Pacing:** Enforced a slow 360ms walking pace and suppressed sprint bursts during the initial 3-second spawn observation window for realistic boss arrival.
- **Administrative Boss Round Test Tools:** Added staff testing commands to trigger dedicated boss special rounds instantly on demand for balance testing and verification.
