[v1.5.9] Navigation Audio, Music Controls & Staff AI Cleanup — 2026-08-02
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Music Bot Companion Controls
- **Personal Music Feed:** Added a shuffled Favorites feed. It keeps the listener's current normal Broadcast or authorized Megaphone routing, so staff can test PA broadcasts while moving between feed songs.
- **Feed Shortcuts:** Added `Ctrl + [` for the previous feed song and `Ctrl + ]` for the next feed song. The ordinary `[` and `]` buffer controls are unchanged.
- **Rebindable Music Bot Controls:** Added the Volume Mixer, Music Bot control, volume-down, and volume-up shortcuts to `Configure key bindings`. The existing `F7`, `M`, `F9`, and `F10` defaults remain unchanged; modifier commands follow the newly selected Music Bot key, the help menu announces the active bindings, and custom Music Bot keys also work while spectating.

• Native Feedback & Platform Independence
- **In-Game Feedback Center:** Added `Send feedback or report a bug` at the end of the authenticated Main Menu, with separate Bug Report, Suggestion, and General Feedback categories plus an accessible history and reply interface.
- **Strict Submission Limits:** Feedback and replies are limited to 1,000 characters. New submissions have a 60-second per-account cooldown and replies have a 10-second cooldown. The Client provides an immediate character limit while the Server independently validates the category, content, identity, ownership, and cooldown.
- **Protected Staff Review:** Moderator-level staff and above receive a separate `Review player feedback` menu and may reply to or resolve open feedback. Builder-only accounts cannot access the review queue.
- **Respectful Reply Notices:** Feedback directions are spoken at most twice per item, and online staff-reply notices are limited to once per minute without dropping any saved replies.
- **Resolve & Archive:** Staff must leave a reply before resolving feedback. Resolved items leave the active queue, remain readable in player history, and are available in a separate staff review list.
- **Protected Permanent Removal:** Only Contributor/Admin-level staff may permanently remove resolved feedback. The action requires a 3–200 character reason, two Client confirmations, and an atomic audit record so a logging failure cannot delete the feedback.
- **Staff Review Guidelines:** Added an inviting staff-only guide covering professional replies, player privacy, fair handling of criticism, responsible resolution, escalation, and the narrow situations where permanent deletion is appropriate.
- **Discord Removal:** Removed the Discord integration, channel configuration, startup/status hooks, and `discord.js` dependency. Feedback and staff notifications now remain inside the game and its persistent database.
- **Compiled Server Resources:** The Server build now copies required Pong JSON resources into `dist/resources`, preventing compiled deployments from stopping during startup because runtime data was missing.

• Client Navigation Audio
- **Wall Proximity Tone:** Added an optional, directional 3D wall cue. While moving forward, backward, or strafing, it checks only the active movement path up to five tiles and smoothly raises pitch as the first wall becomes closer.
- **Compass Direction Check:** Tab now reports the current 16-way compass direction during normal play and provides a spatial `facing.ogg` cue toward the nearest cardinal direction, including while between cardinal headings.
- **Compass Turn Cue:** Added an optional smooth cue that moves left/right with the player's real turning angle. It is disabled by default; the existing post-turn degree announcement is also disabled by default and remains independently configurable.
- **Turning Sensitivity:** Added four persistent turning-speed levels from the game's original turning rate to Very Fast. Highlight the setting in Options and adjust it directly with Left and Right; Enter safely does nothing, and Escape returns to gameplay to try the new speed.

• Staff AI Cleanup
- **Mason AI Broadcast Removal:** Removed the automated Mason AI broadcast timer and the `/aibroadcast` / `/aib` controls. Mason remains available for direct staff assistance and questions.
- **Safe Same-Map Staff Movement:** Fixed the staff `move` command clearing a player's test items, weapons, perks, and related state when only changing coordinates inside the same map. Cross-map moves still perform the established reset so mode-specific equipment cannot leak between maps.

• Custom Online & Offline Sounds
- **Player Profile Sounds:** Added separate custom online and offline sounds tied to each player's profile, with accessible in-game controls to upload, replace, remove, or restore either sound to the game default.
- **How to Upload:** After logging in, open the in-game Options menu, choose `Configure custom online and offline sounds`, then select `Upload online sound` or `Upload offline sound`. Choose an OGG Vorbis file in the file picker and wait for the spoken upload confirmation.
- **Replace or Remove:** File names do not need to match the original game sounds. Uploading another file for the same online or offline slot replaces the current custom sound; choosing `Restore default online sound` or `Restore default offline sound` removes that custom slot and returns it to the built-in game sound.
- **Sound Requirements:** Each file must be OGG Vorbis mono or stereo at 44,100 or 48,000 Hz, between 0.25 and 10 seconds long, and no larger than 512 KB. Online and offline may use different files, or the same file may be uploaded to both slots.
- **Safe Background Delivery:** Custom sounds download and cache outside the main game loop, fall back to the built-in sound when unavailable, and avoid blocking gameplay while players connect or disconnect.
- **Strict Upload Validation:** The server independently validates every upload before linking it to the player's profile, so modified clients cannot bypass the file type, size, audio format, or duration limits.
- **Portable Sound Cache:** Fixed custom sound loading when the game and the Windows user cache are located on different drives.
- **Automatic Radmin VPN Uploads:** Players connected through Radmin VPN can upload or remove custom sounds over the encrypted VPN without configuring HTTPS. The Client permits this exception only when the server uses Radmin's 26.0.0.0/8 range and the player's computer has an active address in that range.
- **Accessible Windows Server Installer:** Added a screen-reader-friendly one-click installer for Radmin-only and public HTTPS deployments. It detects dependencies, prepares persistent sound storage, configures Firewall and automatic startup, and includes a side-effect-free simulation test suite.

🔒 Security & Client Diagnostics
- **Centralized Client Error Reports:** Client exceptions are queued safely and delivered after authenticated login, then retained until the server acknowledges receipt. Full reports are stored per player on the server so staff do not need to request local crash logs.
- **Actionable Staff Alerts:** Error notifications now include a report ID plus the source file, line number, and function. Server logs include bounded diagnostic frames and safe primitive local values while filtering credentials, tokens, chat text, and other sensitive data.
- **Unexpected Exit Recovery:** Per-process session markers detect terminated or unclean client exits on the next launch and attach the available tail of the client debug log without confusing another live game window for a crash.
- **Concurrent Queue Safety:** Crash-report writes are protected across client threads and simultaneous game processes to prevent lost or corrupted reports without blocking normal gameplay.

[v1.5.5] Jupiter Boss Infiltration & Tactical AI Engine Patch — 2026-07-29
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Jupiter Boss & Polymorphic Entity System
- **Jupiter Mini-Boss Infiltration:** Introduced Jupiter boss entities seamlessly into standard Zombie Game Mode rounds starting from Round 5+. Features 10,000 Base HP scaling, 3D arrival announcement roar, isolated 3D spatial footsteps, and administrative spawn commands (max 5 active).
- **Mixed Surprise Encounters:** Starting from Round 16+, Jupiter can now dynamically infiltrate and spawn mixed within standard zombie swarms. It will roar upon arrival but omit the global text announcement, creating a highly dangerous and unexpected jump scare.
- **Continuous Minion Summoning:** When Jupiter's HP drops below 50%, it will roar and instantly summon 2 minion zombies. It will now continuously summon 2 more minions every 25 seconds as long as its HP remains critical, requiring players to focus it down quickly.
- **Squad Reinforcements:** Upgraded the AI helper limit! Players can now deploy up to 4 armed zombie helpers simultaneously, and the starting inventory has been increased to 4 helpers.
- **Polymorphic Audio Engine:** Expanded the entity sound engine to support custom entity sound folders dynamically, suppressing default surface tile footsteps for special boss entities while enabling 3D spatial charge windups and hit sound effects.
- **Seamless Audio Lifecycle:** Extended the entity lifecycle delay upon death to ensure all multi-second death cries and lingering skill sound effects (such as the Thunderstorm) complete playing smoothly without being abruptly cut off by garbage collection.

• Jupiter Boss AI & Tactical Maneuvers
- **Phase 2 Survival Protocol & Absolute Nullification:** Upon reaching critical health, Jupiter now enters a desperate survival phase, instantly generating a massive defensive aura and triggering chaotic, high-speed evasive maneuvers while continuing to cast devastating lightning strikes. Furthermore, the boss is now fully immune to Instakill powerups, immediately shifting into an impenetrable state of absolute immortality to prevent cheap executions!
- **Dynamic 25-Tactic Combat Matrix:** Upgraded Jupiter with an advanced AI tactical decision engine featuring 25 dynamic combat maneuvers. Includes S-curve sweeps, Z-zigzag rushes, spiral envelopes, shield-counter pivots, cover hugging, and 3-second Double Time Sprint bursts with distinct spatial footstep audio.
- **Proactive Melee & Ranged Evasion:** Jupiter now senses incoming melee weapons and firearms in real-time, executing smooth sidesteps and backsteps out of strike zones. During InstaKill mode, Jupiter gains heavy damage resistance (25% max HP cap per hit) and ultra-responsive evasion.
- **Mobile Ultimate Thunderstorm:** Transformed Jupiter's 4-strike thunderbolt ultimate into a mobile casting skill. Jupiter now continues running, sprinting, and chasing targets at full speed while raining down 360-degree lightning strikes.
- **Smooth Arc Trajectory & Audio Panning:** Enhanced AI movement physics with smooth angular LERP curve interpolation, eliminating sharp turn snaps and producing fluid 3D spatial audio panning.
- **360-Degree AoE Melee Sweep:** Transformed the boss's basic melee attack into a full 360-degree area-of-effect sweep, simultaneously damaging all swarming players within range.
- **Phantom Dash Multi-Hit Combo:** Added a devastating new 3-hit combo skill that causes the boss to aggressively leap toward the target, dealing massive AoE cleave damage across 3 consecutive strikes.
- **Dynamic Skill Spacing:** Integrated an intelligent cooldown spacing system that prevents the boss from spamming abilities consecutively, ensuring a tactical 6-second window between ultimate casts for fair counter-play.
- **InstaKill Evasion Refinements:** The AI now completely ignores standard flinch stagger when InstaKill is active, allowing it to seamlessly dodge or aggressively counter-attack (Suan-Kwan) incoming InstaKill strikes.
- **Line-Of-Sight Pathfinding Fallback:** Improved terrain navigation by enforcing line-of-sight validation for tactical maneuvers. Bosses will now automatically fall back to A* pathfinding when obstructed by walls instead of walking aimlessly.
- **Heavy Melee Stagger & Reactive Backstep:** Heavy melee weapon impacts now cause bosses to flinch with a 3D audio groan and sprint back 6–8 tiles, breaking heavy combos while dynamic evasion scales smoothly with weapon reach.
- **Dynamic Multi-Target Engagement:** Upgraded target selection to dynamically transfer aggro to active damage dealers in real-time, providing complete combat freedom during multi-player engagements.
- **Spawn Observation Stance & Sound Boost:** Bosses now observe players for 3 seconds upon arrival before initiating aggressive attacks, while footstep audio is boosted for clear 3D tactical tracking.
- **Audio-Reactive Combat Tactics:** The boss now actively listens to player actions to trigger counter-maneuvers. It will aggressively sprint in a straight line to punish players caught reloading with an empty magazine, and will execute sharp zigzag evasions to dodge incoming fire when detecting sustained gunfire.
- **Enraged Chaos Movement:** When critically wounded (HP < 30%), the boss shifts into a highly erratic "Chaos Relocation" movement pattern, sweeping rapidly from side-to-side to disorient players relying purely on spatial audio.

• Weapons, Equipment & Wallbuys
- **Melee Weapon Wallbuy Refinement:** Improved wallbuy interaction logic for melee weapons. Players will now hear an informative "You already have this weapon" message if they attempt to interact with a melee wallbuy they already own, and the system no longer incorrectly plays the weapon grab sound.
- **Ammo Restock Audio Feedback:** Added a distinct weapon grab sound effect when successfully restocking ammo from a wallbuy to provide clear audio confirmation of the transaction.

• Jupiter Boss Special Rounds & Tactical Variety
- **Dedicated Boss Special Rounds:** Introduced dedicated boss special rounds appearing via probabilistic evaluation (35% chance every 4+ rounds). Features custom announcement broadcasts, clean text formatting, 3D directional vector-boosted spatial arrival roars, and victory rewards (1,000 Bonus Points + Max Ammo + Double Points).
- **Tactical Stalking & Behavioral Variety:** Reduced close-range direct rush frequency, introduced tactical stalking pacing, feint-and-strike counter maneuvers, and a 2.8s–4.5s stance duration window to create organic mind games without constant chasing.
- **Enforced Spawn Stance Pacing:** Enforced a slow 360ms walking pace and suppressed sprint bursts during the initial 3-second spawn observation window for realistic boss arrival.
- **Administrative Boss Round Test Tools:** Added staff testing commands to trigger dedicated boss special rounds instantly on demand for balance testing and verification.

[v0.8.0] The Advanced AI Update — 2026-06-25
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Artificial Intelligence Overhaul
- Introduced 6 distinct AI Difficulty Levels (Beginner to Nightmare).
