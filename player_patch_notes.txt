Beyond Tournament — Player Patch Notes
====================================================================
Every update, summarized for players. Complex system internals and
staff-only tools are left out — the full technical record lives in
changelog.txt.

[v1.7.7] Natural World Map Animals & Spatial Audio Polish — 2026-08-14
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Footsteps and animal sounds now fade out with distance and go fully silent at about 40 steps — no more hearing hoofbeats from across the map.
- Horses and boars on the World Map call out much less often (randomly every 30 seconds to 1 minute) and stagger their first call, so a herd never cries all at once.
- World Map animals behave more naturally: they walk, run, rest and graze at their own random pace and stay close to where they were released.
- Animals can no longer slip through fence corners or jump over pen walls, so they stay inside their stables.

[v1.7.6] Wild Animals, Legendary Bosses & High-Round Scaling — 2026-08-14
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Wild boars roam the World Map and also appear as wild mobs in Zombie matches, with charge attacks.
- New legendary boss: Ares (God of War) — heavy sword strikes, InstaKill resistance and a 360-degree Thunderstorm ultimate.
- Bosses appear at higher rounds now: Jupiter at Round 15+, Minotaur at Round 18+, Dragon at Round 22+, Ares at Round 25+.
- Legendary bosses now spawn only in dedicated boss rounds with clear roars and announcements — no more surprise mixed-wave bosses.
- Clearing boss rounds in high waves awards bonus points scaled to round depth, plus drops like Max Ammo, Double Points and Instakill.
- Bosses and mountable beasts are immune to Nuke instant kills, and Nuke drops are filtered out during boss rounds.
- Reward messages from clearing boss rounds are now saved to the match chat history.
- Fixed horse walk footsteps so walking triggers walk sounds instead of sprint audio.

[v1.7.5] Real-Time Audio & World Map Mountable Horses — 2026-08-14
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Ride horses! Mount, ride and steer standard horses and giant warhorses across the World Map with your interact key (F or Enter).
- Galloping has smooth multi-tier acceleration up to a full cavalry charge.
- High-speed galloping builds a realistic wind-rush sound that fades when you slow down.
- Riders hear a clean, unified hoofbeat rhythm; nearby players hear the full 4-hoof spatial sound.
- Trigger horse whinnies and alert calls on demand with your horn/action key.
- World Map animals wander, graze and rest naturally without targeting players.
- Band mode: any number of performers can broadcast their instruments through the PA at the same time — piano, drums and guitar can all jam together.
- The single music-bot slot stays exclusive, so two songs never play on top of each other.
- PA speakers and voice chat now respond almost instantly (down from ~180ms to ~40ms latency on clean networks).
- Singing over the music or talking while playing no longer glitches or squeezes the audio — each stream mixes cleanly.
- Live cover sync: when a band plays along with the song owner's track, the music lines up in time at the owner's ears.
- Fewer audio stutters on lossy connections.
- Giant Warhorse added as a new horse boss entity.

[v1.7.4] Guitar & Music Bot PA Broadcasting Fix — 2026-08-13
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "Broadcast to Megaphone" now works for the guitar exactly like piano and drums — the toggle alone sends your guitar to the PA.
- Fixed doubled guitar audio (strums no longer play twice for nearby players and through the PA).
- The guitar relay keeps running even when no music track is playing, so a strum broadcasts immediately.

[v1.7.3] Minor Update & Version Sync — 2026-08-13
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- New boss: HorseBeast — horse-themed boss with proactive melee/ranged evasion, sprint bursts and its own neigh, snort, charge and death voice banks.
- Walk and gallop now use separate footstep banks, so running sounds like real galloping instead of stacked clips.

[v1.7.2] Voice Chat, Megaphone Routing & Hotfixes — 2026-08-12
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Speaking into the PA/Megaphone now routes your voice back to yourself with zero latency — you hear yourself instantly.
- Instrument input (guitar/bass line-in) routes through the Megaphone at zero latency for live stage performances.
- Microphone arrays and USB stereo mics are supported; devices that require stereo capture automatically fall back and mix down to mono.
- Fixed a critical bug where the voice encoder silently dropped packets while speaking into the Megaphone (silent broadcasts).
- Staff can make portable voice announcements anywhere on the map again.
- Performers now hear their own piano/drums echoing from the PA exactly like the audience hears.
- Several people can talk through the PA at the same time without clipping or being dropped.
- Talking no longer steals PA routing from a performing pianist/drummer — instruments keep reaching the PA mid-announcement.
- Multiple guitarists can jam through the PA at the same time.
- Pausing briefly (ESC, adjusting volume, chatting) no longer silently drops your PA routing — it only releases when you toggle off, leave the map or disconnect.

[v1.7.0] Multi-Map World Hub, Travel Points, Key Bindings, Guitar Input & Stability Fixes — 2026-08-11
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Fixed frequent client crashes when entering, changing or building maps that contain Pianos and Drumsets.
- Fixed instrument memory leaks across map changes — long sessions no longer accumulate until a crash.
- Server capacity raised to 128 simultaneous players.
- Multi-map world hub: maps can be linked with travel points into a shared world.
- Travel points no longer teleport you automatically — stand in one and press your interact key to travel.
- Travel points show up in the zone check (V).
- The interact key is now the main Enter key by default, and menus show friendly names ("enter" instead of "return").
- New "Clear drum keys" option wipes every custom drum binding at once.
- New "Select instrument input device" setting: your instrument line-in gets its own capture device beside the mic, so you can talk while you play.
- Guitar & bass input (press U): plug in, the game pitch-detects what you play and plays it back at your position instantly.
- Guitar notes broadcast to everyone on the map through the same validated note pipeline as piano and drums.
- Chords and strumming stream on the 3D voice channel, so players nearby hear the full guitar sound.
- Guitar mixes into the Music Bot broadcast when enabled.
- USB effects pedals and amp modellers (Boss GT, Zoom G, Line 6, Valeton, NUX, Mooer, Kemper, etc.) are auto-detected when plugged in.

[v1.6.5] Piano Expression & MIDI Support — 2026-08-08
