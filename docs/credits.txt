music2:  Villainous by David Fesliyan
music3:  Haunted Memories by David Fesliyan
music4:  It Is Coming by David Fesliyan

Original project

Beyond Tournament is built upon Final Hour, an open-source audio game
created by The Lower Elements (Michael Connor Buchan):

- Final Hour client: https://github.com/lower-elements/final-hour-client-public (GPL-3.0)
- Final Hour server: https://github.com/lower-elements/final-hour-server-public (AGPL-3.0)
